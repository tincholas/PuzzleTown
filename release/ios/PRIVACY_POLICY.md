# Privacy Policy for PuzzleTown

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 22, 2026

PuzzleTown ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a cozy, privacy-first logic deduction game. Your privacy is paramount to us, and our application is designed from the ground up to respect your personal data.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, request, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need an account, password, or login to play PuzzleTown.
* **No Location Tracking:** We do not track, access, or record your physical GPS location.
* **No Device Identifiers / IDFA:** We do not track your advertising identifier (IDFA) or engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking.

---

## 2. On-Device Storage (Local Game Data)

PuzzleTown stores game progress exclusively on your local device using standard on-device storage mechanisms:
* Current level progress and unlocked campaign chapters.
* Earned star ratings and puzzle completion records.
* Sound, background music, and vibration preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external servers. You can reset or remove this data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the application.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

* **No Third-Party Advertising:** PuzzleTown contains zero third-party advertisement networks or trackers.
* **No Analytics SDKs:** The native iOS application does not embed third-party behavioral profiling or tracking SDKs.

---

## 4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

PuzzleTown complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
