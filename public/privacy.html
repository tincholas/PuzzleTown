Privacy Policy

PuzzleTown by Stonecall Studio · Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Stonecall Studio ("we", "our", or "us") created PuzzleTown as a cozy, privacy-first logic puzzle game. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to safeguarding your privacy.

🛡️ Summary: PuzzleTown does not collect, sell, or transmit any personal information. All gameplay and save data remain strictly on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe great games should respect your boundaries:

  • No Personal Details: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or accounts.
  • No Account Required: You can play every level completely anonymously without signing in.
  • No Precise Location: We never access or store GPS or location data.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not read or share device advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID).

2. Local On-Device Storage

To preserve your campaign progress, completed levels, star ratings, and sound settings, PuzzleTown stores state locally on your device (via browser localStorage and device storage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to remote servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

PuzzleTown contains zero third-party advertisement networks and zero behavioral ad trackers. The iOS and web games run independently without third-party surveillance SDKs.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PuzzleTown is designed for players of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not collect information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Stonecall Studio's privacy practices, feel free to contact us via our support page or our GitHub repository.
