Frequently Asked Questions

Do I ever need to guess to solve a puzzle?

No! Every single level in PuzzleTown is mathematically verified to be solvable through pure deductive logic. If you are uncertain about a cell, look for other line limits or use the in-game hint button.

Do green grass cells block roads?

No! Marking a tile with green grass simply indicates that no building can be placed there. Roads are free to traverse green grass cells to connect your village network.

How does the hint engine work?

The hint engine breaks deductions down into clear interactive steps: it identifies the premise, highlights candidate tiles, explains why alternative placements are invalid, and guides you to the correct move without spoiling the rest of the board.

Will I lose my progress if I close the app?

No. Your progress, current board state, and earned stars are saved automatically on your device.
